About the concept

Keep the landing page's promise: the case starts on click

Today, after "Try it free"
5 screens

About 1,500 words of accreditation text, then a hub with Duel first, a patient list without the "Opening Case" label, and one more modal.

Concept
1 click

The promised patient, ~8 min, 0.25 credit and the "Start" button on one screen. Disclosures stay before the activity, folded into sections.

Problem

Landings sell one step: an undiagnosed patient in about 8 minutes, no sign-up. The click leads to a wall of disclosures and a choice between modes instead. The company's own published completion rate from the last season is 55%, and this first stretch is where the whole funnel starts.

Hypothesis

If a physician clicking "Try it free" lands straight on the Opening Case card with one primary action, more of them reach the first clinical decision and later register with NPI, because the product matches what the landing promised.

Solution

A single start screen: the patient, three facts (time, credit, no forms), the case path, one "Start the case" button. Accreditation becomes a compact side panel with expandable sections. Name and email are asked only after the debrief, when the credit is already earned. The current hub remains a secondary link.

How to measure

A/B test on the invite link: share of "Try it free" clicks reaching the first decision (primary), Opening Case completion, email capture, NPI entry, and later season completion. Guardrail: disclosure sections opened at least as often as today.
